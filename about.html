Our Story

From a family atelier to a growing American boutique brand.

How We Started

A tradition of heirloom craftsmanship

MIMOS began with a simple belief: children's clothing should be as thoughtfully made as it is beautiful. Drawing on European tailoring traditions, our founders started designing hand-finished pieces for special family moments — christenings, birthdays, holidays and portraits — before growing into a full lifestyle collection for everyday wear.

Today, MIMOS Collections brings that same care to families across the United States, pairing ready-to-ship essentials with fully custom confección pieces made to order.
